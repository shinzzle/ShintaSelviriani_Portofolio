06 / SELECTED PROJECT
INSIGHT CONFERENCE 2026 · PUBLISHED RESEARCH

The Role of Digital Transformation in Mitigating Tax Aggressiveness in Energy and Industrial Companies

This study investigates how digital transformation relates to tax aggressiveness in energy and industrial sector companies listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange. The research uses ETR as the tax aggressiveness measure, DTKeyword as a proxy for digital transformation disclosure, and leverage, profitability, and capital intensity as control variables.

The analysis uses 173 observations from 38 companies and a Random Effects Model with firm-level clustered standard errors. Findings indicate that greater digital transformation disclosure is associated with higher ETR, implying lower tax aggressiveness, with a stronger effect among firms with above-median profitability.
